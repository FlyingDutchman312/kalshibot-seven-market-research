Fixed $5 hypothetical stake, ≥65% confidence and ≥3¢ edge against the selected contract ask. Skips are recorded. Results require officially verified settlement. Gross outcomes use fractional-contract normalization and exclude trading fees, slippage and fill constraints; this is NOT a profitable or executable trading strategy.
